# emotion-match Design Note

## Identity

- `gameId`: `emotion-match`
- Chinese title: 表情小剧场
- Domain: 社会认知
- Task family: facial emotion recognition

## Evidence basis

- Primary paradigm: facial emotion recognition.
- Paper/article references: Ekman basic emotion research and facial affect recognition task literature.
- Open-source/task-library references: jsPsych image/button response patterns; emotion-recognition task implementations as structural reference only.

## MVP mechanics

- Show an original simple face/emotion cue.
- User selects the matching emotion label.
- Trial data records target emotion, response, correctness, RT, and error type.

## Boundary

- Do not copy protected clinical face sets or commercial emotion-test materials.
- Do not claim autism, social cognition disorder, or psychiatric diagnosis.
