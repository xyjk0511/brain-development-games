# gaze-follow Design Note

## Identity

- `gameId`: `gaze-follow`
- Chinese title: 眼神找礼物
- Domain: 社会认知
- Task family: gaze cueing / joint attention

## Evidence basis

- Primary paradigm: gaze cueing and joint attention.
- Paper/article references: Posner cueing paradigm and Friesen & Kingstone gaze cueing literature.
- Open-source/task-library references: jsPsych spatial cueing/visual response patterns.

## MVP mechanics

- Show a central character and two side objects.
- The character's gaze/arrow cues left or right.
- User selects the attended side.
- Trial data records cue direction, target side, response, correctness, RT, and cue validity.

## Boundary

- Use as a gameified social cue-reading task.
- Do not claim it is a clinical joint-attention assessment.
