# social-scenario Design Note

## Identity

- `gameId`: `social-scenario`
- Chinese title: 小镇小故事
- Domain: 社会认知
- Task family: social inference / theory of mind

## Evidence basis

- Primary paradigm: social inference and theory-of-mind scenario reasoning.
- Paper/article references: Theory of Mind, social problem solving, emotion inference task literature.
- Open-source/task-library references: jsPsych survey/button response patterns; original scenarios only.

## MVP mechanics

- Present a short everyday social scenario.
- User selects a likely feeling, need, or appropriate next action.
- Trial data records scenario cue type, correct answer, response, correctness, RT, and error type.

## Boundary

- Do not reuse protected clinical items such as Reading the Mind in the Eyes or Faux Pas materials.
- Do not claim clinical diagnosis; keep wording at training/practice level.
